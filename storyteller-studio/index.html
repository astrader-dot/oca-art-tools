  • Drawing tools: Pencil is grainy like graphite (pick a color for colored pencil). Ink makes a thick line when you move slowly and a thin one when you move fast. Brush is see-through, like ink wash or watercolor. Fine liner makes a clean, even line. Smooth and Thick are for painting.
  • Shade brushes on halftone dots, crosshatching, or stippling in Light, Medium, or Dark. Turn on Paint inside (Layers tab) to shade only inside a shape. To fill a whole area with shading, set Fill to Pattern.
  • Painting with a trackpad? Press down and hold while you drag. Let go to finish the stroke.
  • Pages are next to the canvas. Comic and Wordless stories can have up to 3 pages. Click a page to work on it, or click Add page for a new blank one. Use the arrows under Move page to put the page you're on earlier or later.
  • Panels (Comic and Wordless): use the bar above the page to pick 3 to 6 panels and a layout. Paint stays inside the panel you start in, so the gutters between panels stay clean.
  • Changing the layout never deletes paint. Anything that ends up in a gutter is hidden, and it comes back if you change the layout again.
  • Words (in the toolbar) adds speech balloons, thought balloons, and captions in a Comic. Wordless stories can use captions only. Click the page to add one, then type in the box on the right.
  • To move a balloon, use Words and drag it. Drag the square to make it wider, and drag the purple dot to point the tail at whoever is talking.
  • Wordless pages can be one Full page picture or use Panels. Switch with the buttons above the page.
  • Click Panel in the corner of the canvas to zoom in on the panel you're working on. Use the arrows to move from panel to panel.
  • Each page has three layers. Start on Background with big shapes. Switch to Middle for your main subject and Details for highlights, edges, and small marks.
  • Open Lights and darks under the color wheel for lighter and darker versions of your colors. Using the light and dark versions of a color is how you show depth.
  • Turn on Paint inside in the Layers tab to shade or add highlights inside a shape without going over its edges.
  • Click Watch my process in the More tab to see a time-lapse of your work. It's great for your reflection.
  • Blend smudges paint on the selected layer only. Fill pours color into the area you see. Switch Fill to Gradient and drag across the canvas for a smooth blend between two colors on the Background layer, like a sky.
  • Use + and − in the corner of the canvas to zoom in for small details. When you're zoomed in, turn on Move and drag to look around, or scroll with two fingers on a trackpad. Click the percent to see the whole page again.
  • Typed Story: click Story in the Pages column to type your story. The meter under it shows whether it fits on one page. Click Picture to paint your illustration.
  • My story plan (at the top) is optional. Copy your three-act map there to keep it handy while you work.
  • Read my story (More tab) shows your story one panel at a time, the way a reader sees it.
  • Picked the wrong format? Click Format at the top to switch. Switching never deletes your work.
  • Your story saves on this device as you work. Switching computers? Use Save backup file at the top, keep the file in your Art Folder, and open it on the other computer.
  • Download PDF puts all your pages into one file with your name on it. To add a process page showing how your story grew, turn it on in the More tab.
  • Shortcuts: Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, [ and ] change brush size, + and − zoom, 0 shows the whole page.
