Already finished the Painting Theme Builder? You can skip this and paint using the choices you wrote down there. Fill it in only if you want to: the studio will build your color palette, mark your brush with a ★, and print your choices under your painting when you download it.

  • Start on the Background layer with big shapes. Switch to Middle for your main subject and Details for highlights, edges, and small marks.
  • If you choose a brushwork in My painting plan, that brush gets a ★ in the toolbar. You can use any brush, but your choice should show the most.
  • Open Lights and darks under the color wheel for lighter and darker versions of your colors. Using the light and dark versions of a color is how you show depth.
  • In the Check tab, turn on Value check to see your painting in grays, plus a meter of your darks, middles, and lights. If everything looks like the same gray, add more lights and darks.
  • Turn on Paint inside in the Layers tab to shade or add highlights inside a shape without going over its edges.
  • Click Watch my painting in the Check tab to see a time-lapse of your work. It's great for your reflection.
  • Blend smudges paint on the selected layer only. Fill pours color into the area you see. Switch Fill to Gradient and drag across the canvas for a smooth blend between two colors on the Background layer, like a sky.
  • Canvas texture (in the Check tab) adds the look of real canvas fabric. It shows in your downloaded painting too.
  • Your painting saves on this device as you work. Switching computers? Use Save backup file at the top, keep the file in your Art Folder, and open it on the other computer.
  • Shortcuts: Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, [ and ] change brush size.
